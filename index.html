Dojun Park

Computational linguist

Hi, I'm Dojun. I study how language models interpret meaning, infer intentions, and use context across languages. My work focuses on computational pragmatics, multilinguality, and LLM evaluation. I aim to connect these insights to the development of more capable language models, bringing linguistic analysis into model development and evaluation.
